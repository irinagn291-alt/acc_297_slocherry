Version 1.0. First release of Slocherry, a looking quiz that seats a circular excerpt on a full work already saved.

• Three-page intro: Lodge the tondo, Cut then lodge, Keep your crate, then the field.
• Field home with status Waiting, Crop ready, Filed or Missed; circular excerpt; four full grounds; Filed and Missed counts.
• Cut punches a tondo from a Loose work when four or more works are saved.
• Lodge by tapping the panel that owns the excerpt; a miss writes a ChipMark and the excerpt stays.
• Explore the J. Paul Getty Museum, Search a work, Save as Loose and a local shelf when search is empty or misses.
• Saved crate of Filed excerpts and Missed guesses; Open this work for the Getty record.
• Settings: Progress, Collection (J. Paul Getty Museum and Open Content Program), Undo, Re-run onboarding, Reset the field, Contact.
• System Shortcuts: Open Quiz, Open Explore, Open Saved, Open Settings, Cut a tondo, Lodge the tondo.
• Portrait, light appearance, iPhone and iPad, iOS 17.0. English copy. Counts and dates follow the device locale.

No sign-in. Works, marks and onboarding state stay on this device.
